Under the hood

What makes it work

The answers come from official documents, not from the model's memory.

Retrieval

Search by meaning

Documents are split into chunks and embedded with multilingual-e5-small. FAISS finds the closest ones, in German or English.

Questions

Handles vague ones

Query expansion, cross-topic search and ambiguity handling help with short or multi-part questions.

Model

Runs locally

Qwen2.5 writes the answer from the retrieved text. 1.5B for local development, 7B for production.

Speed

Tuned for latency

Up to 150 new tokens, 600 characters per chunk, top 2 chunks, repetition penalty 1.1.
